Our Story

The Club.

Tinsukia Tennis Club is more than just a place to hit a ball. It is the heart of grassroots tennis in Upper Assam — built on the belief that tennis should be accessible, competitive, and community-driven.

Founded by local tennis enthusiasts, TTC has grown from a handful of players to a vibrant community spanning all ages and skill levels. Our focus has always been twofold: developing junior talent to compete at the highest levels, and providing a welcoming environment for recreational players to enjoy the game.

We are proud to host major regional tournaments, run comprehensive coaching programs, and maintain some of the finest playing facilities in the district.
